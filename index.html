The Process

Your Website
in 5 steps

Our process ensures that we create a website tailored to your business needs.

01
Do we Match?

Discovery Call

Before we start, we determine if and how I can help you. What are your requirements for your new website? Why do you need a new website? What goals do you have, and what problems can we solve with a new website?

02
Planning & Strategy

Concept & Strategy

Together, we develop a strategy that effectively addresses your target audience and goals. Based on the strategy, I create a concept for your website that is not only visually appealing but also functional.

03
The Magic

Design & Development

I begin to design and develop your website using Webflow, a powerful no-code tool. I keep you in the loop with regular updates, and you can share feedback along the way.

04
Optimization

Testing & Optimization

I thoroughly test your website and optimize it for all devices & screen sizes. I also optimize performance, SEO and accessibility before launch.

05
Go Live

Launch & Support

After everything is tested and optimized, we go live. I also offer ongoing support and maintenance to ensure your website continues to perform.
